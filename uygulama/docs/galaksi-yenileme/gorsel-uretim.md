# Yerleşik Imagegen üretim yönergeleri

İki görsel yeni üretildi; CLI/API anahtarı kullanılmadı. Üretilen PNG dosyaları yeniden boyutlandırılmadan proje içine kopyalandı. İstenen ideal boyut ile elde edilen boyut aynı değildir: her iki sonuç da 1774×887 pikseldir.

## galaxy-panorama.png

`transparent_background: false`

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production texture for a romantic cinematic interactive 3D galaxy. Asset: seamless equirectangular celestial nebula panorama, 360 degrees longitude, 180 latitude, 2:1 aspect, ideally 3840x1920. Entire image is outer space, no horizon or planets, NO text UI letters symbols. Refined Hubble deep space astrophotography blended with colorful space art. Detailed translucent wisps and branching ionized gas filaments, smoky volumetric dust cavities, incredibly fine turbulence across multiple scales. NOT rocks, NOT mountains, NOT cracked lava. Deep near-black navy negative spaces cover 45 percent. A sweeping asymmetric nebula river runs from upper left through lower center and toward right: electric cobalt and violet at upper left, rose magenta localized fine emission rims, luminous pale turquoise and subtle emerald gold at lower center right. The brightest cyan-gold gas with backlit dark dust cores is around 65 percent across and 60 percent down; upper center and far left more open dark blue space. Rich color but preserve photographic subtlety and deep shadows, no broad white glare, no uniform blue fog. Only very tiny restrained distant stars, no giant star flares. Soft natural continuity across left/right edges and dark low-contrast poles suitable for spherical projection. No borders, no rectangular patches, no obvious repeating patterns. Highest detailed premium cinematic texture.

## galaxy-veil.png

`transparent_background: true`

Use case: stylized-concept. Production VFX sprite texture, wide landscape 2:1. A single expansive extremely delicate translucent interstellar emission nebula wisp floating on TRUE TRANSPARENT background. Entire outer border fully transparent. Photographic Hubble gaseous material, no solid surfaces. A sweeping asymmetric loose ribbon of vapor stretching diagonally from lower left to upper right with fine branching frayed tendrils, open transparent holes and dark smoky dust channels between filaments. Wispy soft low density cobalt blue and violet gas with thin pale cyan illuminated rims, localized tiny rose-magenta glow and subtle warm champagne. Restrained luminance. It must look like translucent fine smoke and cosmic gas, NOT rocky, NOT mountains, NOT lava, NOT coral, NOT lightning, NOT a solid cutout object. No stars, no bright central orb, no planets, no text. Exceptionally detailed fine turbulent vapor texture with soft transparent tapering all around. Designed to be composited at low opacity in a 3D scene as a foreground parallax veil; dark masses also partly transparent, no rectangular backdrop.
